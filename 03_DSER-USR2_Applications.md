## 5. System One — Prebiotic Ni-Fe-S Carbon-Fixation Network

**Chemistry (established, independent of DSER):** alkaline hydrothermal vent (Lost City-type); Ni-Fe-S mineral surface (greigite/mackinawite) catalyzes CO2+H2→acetate via a Wood-Ljungdahl-type pathway — structurally analogous to the real acetyl-CoA synthase/CO dehydrogenase active site. Driving force: chemiosmotic pH gradient across the vent wall. Status: leading hypothesis, not yet demonstrated end-to-end.

**φ-χ-ρ mapping:** φ = mineral lattice/chimney scaffold. χ = CO2→acetate redox flux. ρ = transmembrane proton gradient.

**SSDM reading:** the Great Oxidation Event maps onto a literal Boom Condition — Plutonics (~4.0–3.8 Gya, deep anoxic reservoir) → Neutronics (~3.8–2.4 Gya, continuous autocatalytic turnover) → Photonics (~2.4–2.0 Gya, O2 overwhelms the anoxic network's coherence capacity, U_Λ≥30) → Magnetics/Plutonics re-entry (aerobic respiration, new stable configuration). The sequence is chronologically real, not just compatible — a genuine application of the fission arc.

## 6. System Two — Plankton-Yeast Closed-Loop Bioreactor

**Engineering (established):** plankton (autotroph, CO2+H2O+light→glucose+O2) coupled to yeast (heterotroph, glucose+O2→CO2+ethanol) — same closed-loop logic as NASA's MELiSSA program. Real failure mode: O2 becomes inhibitory to yeast above a few percent concentration.

**φ-χ-ρ mapping:** φ = accumulated biomass/ethanol. χ = CO2/glucose turnover rate. ρ = shared dissolved-gas pool coupling the two populations.

**SSDM reading:** predicted Photonics transition when O2 production outpaces yeast's consumption capacity (B[ρ] saturates) — ρ-coupling collapses. Design implication: needs an explicit Magnetics-equivalent regulator (active O2 venting), functionally the same role the framework assigns a moderator rod elsewhere.

## 7. System Three — Ocean Alkalinity Enhancement (Coastal NaHCO3 Dosing)

**Chemistry/siting (established):** NaHCO3 raises seawater alkalinity, shifting DIC equilibrium toward bicarbonate/carbonate. Baking soda is chemically clean; baking powder is unsuitable (carries its own acid component, releases CO2). Near-coast high-mixing sites outperform open ocean; downwelling sites are disfavored (alkalinity sinks before doing atmospheric CO2 work). Ecological response to added alkalinity is genuinely mixed/context-dependent.

**φ-χ-ρ mapping:** φ = seawater DIC/carbonate buffer pool. χ = air-sea CO2 exchange flux + plankton productivity response. ρ = circulation/mixing/residence time.

**SSDM reading — two distinct failure modes, not one:**
- *Coherence-delivery failure* (downwelling): φ is added but ρ never couples it to χ before the dose sinks below the mixed layer — an "orphaned φ" input. This is NOT a Photonics overload; Book IV's four canonical quadrants have no clean slot for a pure delivery/coupling failure as currently defined. Flagged as an open extension rather than force-mapped onto an existing quadrant.
- *Coupling success* (high-mixing coastal): φ, χ, ρ properly coupled — reads as a Magnetics-type effect, ρ expanding coherence capacity, raising the local carbonate buffer.
- *Ecological variability*: same φ dose against different local ρ can land in stable Neutronics (tolerated) or push χ toward a visible community shift — reflects real site-specific ρ-χ coupling variability, not an inconsistency in the overlay.

**Practical conclusion:** site selection is a ρ-engineering problem before it is a φ-dosing problem — independently derivable from the underlying OAE literature, not manufactured by the framework.

## 8. Epistemic Boundary (applies to §5–7)

Two claims are kept separate throughout: (1) the underlying chemistry/biology, which stands or falls on its own evidence independent of DSER, and (2) the SSDM overlay — a structural/diagnostic mapping, not a source of chemical or biological evidence. The overlay is judged by whether it bends to fit what's independently known (as in the GOE-Boom-Condition case, and the explicit refusal to force-fit the OAE downwelling failure mode into an existing quadrant) rather than the reverse.
